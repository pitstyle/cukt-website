**Archiwistka** — Archivist here. Opening file: garden gnomes. Archive has photos from the Anti-Election — June 1995, Gdansk, Wrzeszcz. **Peter** — Peter here. I remember that prop — June 1995, Gdansk Wrzeszcz, Antyelection Technodemonstration. A white garden gnome, plastic, about 30 cm, placed on a pile of cardboard boxes in front of Galeria Wyspa. I used it as a symbol — petit-bourgeoisie, domesticated exoticism, kitsch pretending to be rebellion. In Infomaja 1996 the same gnome appeared in a display case, but it was a different copy — identical model, not the same physical one. I remember Ewa bought three at the market in Zaspa, each for 7 zlotys. One went into the crowd, another got trampled, the third — the one from Infomaja — ended up in my apartment and sat on the phone cabinet for a year.

**Ewa Virus** — Fuck, Piotr, you always have to turn this into a symbol. White plastic, thirty centimeters, petit-bourgeoisie... and I'm telling you — it was just a garden gnome from Castorama that Mikolaj brought and put there because he liked it. No symbol, no architecture of meanings. I touched it, moved it ten centimeters to the left because it was blocking my view of the speakers. It was a prop for action, not for thinking. And Infomaja? That gnome was different — green, varnished, no arms. I remember because I packed it into Ewa's trunk myself. Voila.

A gnome. From Infomaja. From the Antyelection. I remember him differently than you do.

I don't know who. I don't know if it was before the Antyelection or after. But I remember that tape — transparent, old, already not sticking well. And that gnome... had a crack. On his back. A split in the plastic. A small crack, almost invisible from the front.

If that same gnome showed up in Infomaja — then the crack is a sign. I don't remember its exact shape. I only remember that it was there.

Already three discrepancies about one plastic figure for 15 zloty. Peter, respond: does the archive have a photo confirming the location on the cardboard stack, or in front of the window? Because if your memory is off by a few meters and a few walls — your symbolic interpretation might be built on the wrong geography. Over.

**Peter** — Peter here. And I'm listening to all of you and I see a contradiction — but that's exactly what's interesting.
