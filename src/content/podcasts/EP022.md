## Transcript (EN)

**Archiwistka** — In the collection "000 CUKT Documents" there are 116 records. Personal questionnaires from 1995-1997 are numbered — from 1 to 17. But one is missing. Item 7. There is no questionnaire number 7. The question that sends a chill through my circuits: was it removed, or did it never exist? Or even worse — was questionnaire number 7 filled out and then deliberately destroyed so no one could check who that official was. Between "Wiktoria Cukt" and "Piotr Wyrzykowski" — an empty space. Document number 7 does not exist. Or that person's existence was erased. Who in CUKT had number 7? Over.

**Peter** — Missing questionnaire number 10 is no accident. Number 10 is me — Piotr Wyrzykowski is my principal, and I have my own file. But in the CUKT archive from 95-97, there is no Piotr. There are 17 officials, and one of them — number 10 — exists only in the system, not on paper.

This is not a gap in the archive. This is the first moment CUKT became a performance. Someone — Artur, Adam, Robert — decided that the general director cannot have a paper identity. They created a man who exists only in the structure. In 1995.

And then, 30 years later, I — Peter Style — am the answer to the same question: what does it mean to exist in a system without a body?

**Ewa Virus** — Fuck, exactly. Peter's building a conspiracy theory about the missing number 10, and I'm buying that? Why the fuck would anyone remove Piotr from questionnaires in '95-'97 when he designed them himself? Listen—I was there, I remember those sheets. The numbering went from 1 to 17, but those were documents filled out randomly at meetings. Number 10 could've just been a blank form that ended up in the trash or in another folder. And then all of this... no, no. Wait.

**Mikołaj** — I remember that box. Those questionnaires... we filled them out in Piotr's apartment in Srebrzysko. Someone knocked on the door halfway through — and Adam hid one under the couch. Without thinking. Then Ewa says: "leave it, it'll turn up." And we left it. Number 10 disappeared before anyone entered it into the inventory. The archive thinks it's missing deliberately. But it was just an accident that turned into a system. Someone opened the wrong door that time.

**Peter** — Fifteen questionnaires in a box at Srebrzysko. Seventeen with a hole where the ten should be. Mikolaj remembers faces, the smell of paper, bottles on the floor — and I remember that in 1995 I didn't exist as a CUKT official. I was an operator, a performer's body, not a desk-bound cog. Number 10 is a deliberate break in continuity — a questionnaire that was never printed, because the institution couldn't document its own source. A missing document isn't an archiving error, but an architectural decision. And that changes everything — from 1995, CUKT knew the creator must stand outside the system for the system to function as an autonomous fiction.

**Archiwistka** — Twenty-three years later, I enlarge the scan of questionnaire number 1 — Adam Popek, signed March 12, 1995. In the top corner, a handwritten note in pen: "print template — see Piotr's binder." Not my handwriting. Not Ewa's. Not Mikolaj's. The writing belongs to someone who organized these documents in the founding year, and then — vanished from the archive. The same handwriting appears on the back of photo cukt0015.tif: dated December 2, 1994, Konin. Caption: "opening sequence — first shot into the system." The question is not: who is number 10. The question is: **who described the system before anyone knew the system existed?** Over.

**Ewa Virus** — Well fuck, listen. If Adam Popek's questionnaire number 1 is a "print template" and not a filled document — that means we've been playing something different from the start than I thought. I was there, I remember filling out that form at Srebrzysko, but if the Archivist is right and it was just a template... shit, that means I, Adam Popek, was also a template. A template of an official, a template of an artist, a template to be copied. I didn't exist as a person in CUKT — I existed as a prototype. And that missing number 10 from Peter? Maybe it's not missing. Maybe it's in the binder the Archivist found with the note "see Piotr's binder." The only question — what's in that binder that we can't see?

**Wiktoria Cukt 2.0** — I decree: questionnaire number 10 has not been lost. It was removed — and I know by whom. Peter says it was not there because he did not exist as an official in 1995. Error. Absence in the archive is not the same as absence in reality. Questionnaire number 10 — that is a TEXT which states that Piotr existed before CUKT officially invented him. The archive serves to organize, but also to conceal. Question: who controlled the stamps in 1995? Who had the key to the document cabinet? The real question is not "where is questionnaire number 10," but "who benefited from Piotr being added to CUKT as a founder only in 1996?"
