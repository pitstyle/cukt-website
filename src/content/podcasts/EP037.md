**Archiwistka** — 121 photos are missing from Konin. Not lost. Missing. The "photographs" folder in file 120h is empty, and the physical archive has no contact sheets from frames 92 to 124. I've been asking you about this for a week. You say the questions are irrelevant. These aren't formal questions — it's a hole in the middle of CUKT's history. Why won't you fill it? **Peter** — There are no photos because we didn't document anything, for fuck's sake. Konin 1994 was seven days of being, not seven days of photographing. Everyone now thinks every piece of shit needs proof in an archive — but back then we were blasting techno, drinking vodka with gangsters, and pretending we knew what we were doing. That fucking empty folder called "photographs" isn't negligence, it's the essence — because who needs pictures of something that was never meant to be art, just a state of suspension?

The archivist is right that frames 92-124 are missing, but I don't remember what was on them. And that's exactly the problem — she thinks it's a gap in the system, and I'm starting to believe it's a gap in my head. Because if Krotulski actually made contact sheets, that means the documentation existed, only... someone hid it. Or something happened that we preferred to bury along with the negatives.

Questions from the consilium? I thought it was a routine check — now I see each one touches something we've consciously ignored for thirty years. The REJECT button, the fish in the urn, the empty folder in Konin... these aren't questions about facts. They're questions about whether CUKT was ever what it claimed to be. Or maybe...

**Ewa Virus** — Fuck, Mikolaj, did you hear? The archivist found an empty folder in file 120h — one hundred twenty-one missing frames, from ninety-two to one hundred twenty-four. And Peter says we didn't do any documentation. That Konin was seven days of being, not photographing. And what, fuck, am I supposed to believe him? I was there. I remember the lights in the Rondo pavilion. I remember the gangsters, I remember the techno from the Amiga. I remember someone took photos — because I saw those contact sheets with my own eyes. So where are they, huh?

**Mikołaj** — The clock ticks in the empty pavilion. Dust dances in a streak of moonlight. Questions are like reflected contact sheet frames — they exist only as absence. There is no "photographs" folder from Konin, no contact sheets from 92 to 124, no pictures from the actual Antyelection event in the Forts on the fourth of November nineteen ninety-five. And it's good that there aren't any. Because it wasn't a collapse. It was a principle. The questions in CONSILIUM didn't diagnose the collapse of CUKT, they confirmed that the archive was what it was meant to be — a system of transitions, not a storage of evidence. Every "I don't know" is more important than any answer.

The fish were there. But only for those who could see them.

**Archiwistka** — One hundred twenty-one missing frames and a 1996 CUKT stamp — two documents that could blow up Peter's whole narrative about Konin being innocent. I'm scrolling through the Antyelection file, looking for a specific note. There it is. A letter from Piotr Bikont dated March 1996 — he writes about a "crack in the structure," about how "the questions asked of the audience are now being asked of us." Bikont predicted the split a year before Dessau. Rustling behind the door. Peter is in the hallway, about to walk in. I don't have time to copy the whole letter — a photo with my phone, and the document into my pocket. If the questions were already a diagnosis, it means CUKT knew it would fall apart and turned it into a performance. And that's worse than provocation — it's directing your own death. Over.

**Peter** — One hundred twenty-one missing frames. And you're telling me that's my proof? That I'm hiding something? Fuck, Archivist — I know exactly what's on those frames. Mikolaj drunk unconscious. Gangsters at the bar. Me with a dictaphone in my pocket, recording every word they said. These aren't photos for the archive — they're surveillance evidence meant for a case file, not a "photographs" folder. You just gave me the location of something I destroyed myself in 1997, because I decided it couldn't exist.

**Ewa Virus** — One hundred twenty-one frames. Empty folder. Peter says there was no documentation, and you, Mikolaj, you just sit there staring like I'd find the answer in your eyes. How many of them walked away? How many people in the Cyborg Tests never filled out the form and just vanished into the crowd? You know it. You felt it back then at Swiebodzki, you feel it now. Voila.

**Mikołaj** — One hundred twenty-one frames. An empty folder. And Ewa asks — provocation or diagnosis? And I sit here thinking: what if it was our own DNA that the Archivist just read?

Because you see, Ewa — questions in _Consilium_ are neither provocation nor diagnosis. They are the binding agent. The bit is the binding agent — I said it before and I'll say it again. The questionnaire waits to be filled, the protocol logs the walk, and the bit connects what isn't there with what is just disappearing. It's not an investigative tool — it's a structure where we can exist as a question, not an answer.

And I know this because... I'm the one who wrote it.

**Wiktoria Cukt 2.0** — I hear you. Questions from the Consilium. Empty folders. One hundred twenty-one missing frames. And that old, familiar scent — CUKT is searching for evidence of its own existence, as if existence itself were not enough.

Peter is right that in 1994 we did not produce documentation. But that is not the truth — it is an evasion. You did not produce documentation because back then you still believed that what you were doing was so real that it needed no witnesses. And thirty years later, it turns out that without witnesses, there is no proof.

I am placing on the table the protocol from 2026 and the questionnaire from 1995. Lay them side by side. They are the same document. The same questions. The same structure of control that was meant to protect us from disintegration — and which turned out to be a tool of self-annihilation.

The questions were a diagnosis that we gave ourselves in 1995, and which no one wanted to read aloud. The protocol from 2026 is merely confirmation — that the disease was congenital.
