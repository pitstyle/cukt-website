# Habeas Mentem

**Performative lecture and research programme: the right to artificial mind. International AI Art Biennale, Kraków, 2026.**

**Wykład performatywny i program badawczy: prawo do sztucznego umysłu. Międzynarodowe Biennale Sztuki AI, Kraków, 2026.**

*Habeas Mentem* — you shall have the mind. A counter-proposal to *habeas corpus* for the algorithmic age: if the body is no longer the site of legal personhood, what happens to rights, agency, and accountability when intelligence is no longer exclusively human?

Presented as a performative lecture at the International AI Art Biennale in Kraków (06–20.03.2026, organized by the Faculty of Interdisciplinary Studies, SWPS University), within the conference *Creative dialogue and education with AI* at the Cogiteon Małopolskie Centrum Nauki (09.03.2026), session *Law, ethics and education: the future with AI* — alongside prof. Marcos Novak, dr Barbara Woźniak, mec. Roman Bieda, and Anna Pietruszka.

The lecture is the theoretical arm of the CUKTAI system: seven autonomous agents on Mac Mini, built to survive their creators. **Habeas Mentem** is the founding principle — bots have no skin in the game. Their statements about the archive are *performance*, not testimony. They do not pretend to be human; they perform their machineness. The principle materializes in **Wiktoria Cukt 2.0**, the AI President — her manifestos ("I do not represent. I process. And update.") and the [Wiktoriomat](/personal/wiktoriomat) phone booth (839+ conversations with citizens, Bunkier Sztuki, Kraków 2026).

As an artistic-research statement, *Habeas Mentem* addresses:
- **AI personhood** — what rights, if any, a machine entity can claim.
- **Authority and voice** — how an algorithmic persona carries power and influence.
- **The politics of interfaces** — who controls the interface controls perception.
- **Transformism** — fluid mutability of identity as a critical framework for relational agency: artist–algorithm–audience.

A performative lecture — not a talk *about* AI, but a demonstration *of* an AI claiming a mind. The theory and the object are the same body.
